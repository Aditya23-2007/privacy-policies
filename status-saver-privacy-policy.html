Privacy Policy for Status Saver

Effective Date: August 11, 2026
Application Name: Status Saver
Package Name: com.appfactory.saver

1. Overview

Status Saver ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles your information when you use our WhatsApp Status Saver utility.

2. Information Collection and Storage

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data (such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, contacts, or chat messages).
  • Local Storage Access Only: Status Saver accesses media files (photos and videos) stored in your device's WhatsApp status folder (.Statuses) solely to display and save them to your local device gallery.
  • No Remote Servers: All status media downloaded or saved using this app remains strictly on your local device storage. We do not upload your media to any external servers or cloud services.

3. Permissions Used

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: To view WhatsApp status photos and videos stored locally on your device.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / MediaStore API: To save selected status photos and videos to your device's Pictures/Status Saver gallery folder.
  • Storage Access Framework (SAF): To request explicit user permission to read the hidden status directory in compliance with Android Scoped Storage guidelines.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To load Google AdMob advertisements.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Status Saver uses Google AdMob to display advertisements inside the app. Google AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) and non-personal diagnostic data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.

5. Third-Party Brand Disclaimer

Status Saver is an independent media utility tool and is NOT affiliated, associated, authorized, endorsed by, or in any way officially connected with WhatsApp LLC, WhatsApp Business, or Meta Platforms, Inc.
